The Story

Why Does the North Sound Like This?

Scandinavia produces a disproportionate share of the world's most-streamed music. From ABBA to Avicii, from A-ha to Aurora — something in the Nordic temperament translates into sound that resonates globally.

It's the contrast. The darkness and the light. The restraint and the euphoria. The fjord and the festival. Nordic artists have learned to channel extremes into music that feels both deeply personal and universally human.

Midnightsun.fm exists to surface these artists — the ones you know, and the ones you should.
